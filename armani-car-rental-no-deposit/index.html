Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about renting, deposits, documents, and driving in Dubai.

What documents do I need to rent a car in Dubai? +

For Tourists: A valid passport, UAE entry/visit visa stamp, and a valid national driving licence. Visitors from countries requiring an International Driving Permit (IDP) must also present an IDP.

For UAE Residents: Valid Emirates ID, UAE Driving Licence, and a residential visa.

Do you offer free delivery to hotels and Dubai Airport? +

Yes! We offer free delivery and collection across Dubai (including DXB Airport, Dubai Marina, Downtown, Palm Jumeirah, and all hotels) for rentals of 3 days or more. Delivery is also available for shorter periods for a minimal fee.

Is a security deposit required and when is it refunded? +

Most vehicles require a refundable security deposit to cover potential RTA traffic fines or Salik tolls. Many economy and monthly rentals come with zero-deposit options. All security deposits are processed and released within 21 to 30 days as per UAE regulations.

What is the included daily mileage allowance? +

Each rental includes a generous standard allowance of 250 km to 300 km per day. Weekly and monthly packages include proportional bulk allowances. Additional kilometres are billed at transparent rates clearly stated on your contract.

Can I rent a car on a monthly basis? +

Absolutely. Our monthly rentals feature discounts of up to 40% compared to daily rates, free routine maintenance, complimentary replacement vehicles, and zero long-term commitments.
