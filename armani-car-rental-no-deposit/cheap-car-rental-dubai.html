Frequently asked questions

What do I need to rent a car in Dubai?

Tourists need a passport, entry stamp and a valid driving licence (an International Driving Permit may be needed). Residents need an Emirates ID and a valid UAE or foreign licence.

Is a security deposit required?

Most cars need a refundable deposit. Many economy and mid-range cars have zero-deposit options, especially on weekly and monthly rentals.

Do you deliver the car to my hotel or the airport?

Yes. We deliver and collect across Dubai, including DXB airport, hotels and homes. Delivery is free on rentals of 3 days or more.

Is insurance included?

Basic comprehensive insurance is included with every rental. Optional full cover can be added to reduce the excess to zero.
